    Research basis

    Scheduling uses the open FSRS (Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler) Difficulty–Stability–Retrievability model descended from:

    Ye, J., Su, J., & Cao, Y. (2022). A Stochastic Shortest Path Algorithm for Optimizing Spaced Repetition Scheduling. KDD 2022. doi:10.1145/3534678.3539081 · paper site: maimemo.com/paper

    This demo implements FSRS-4.5 update formulas (default weights) for interval scheduling in the browser. It does not reproduce the paper’s SSP-MMC optimizer experiments or claim MaiMemo’s 12.6% lift — those need large review logs. Honest scope: faithful DSR scheduling math + everyday UX.
